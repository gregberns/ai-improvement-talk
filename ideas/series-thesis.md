# Series Thesis

Drafted 2026-04-17 while working on article 017. This feels like the thesis of the whole series, not just one article. Parking here to develop further — could become the series intro, could anchor the framing at multiple points.

---

There are two modes. If you don't use the right one in the right places, you'll see sub-optimal performance. Conversely, the more you use the right mode for the right tasks, the better performance you'll see.

By breaking the development process down and using the right language during a phase, we can get the agent into the right mode.

With small steps that are high-performing, they can link together into highly performant processes that are reliable, catch errors earlier, validate themselves, and take the tedium out of babysitting agents.
