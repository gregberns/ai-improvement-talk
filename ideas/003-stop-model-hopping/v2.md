# Stop Model-Hopping. Best Model, Max Effort.

**Category:** `[foundation]`

## 1. Symptom

"Tokens are expensive." "I want something cheaper." "I like OSS." "I'm using Copilot/Cursor/whatever because it's included." "I tried Claude once and it was bad, so I went back to completions."

You're bouncing between models, optimizing for cost, or settling for whatever came bundled with your IDE.

## 2. What to Change

Stop model-hopping. Best available model. Maximum effort setting. Tune it in.

Right now that means Claude Opus at HIGH effort. Tomorrow it might be something else. The principle: always run the best, never count tokens.

> We'll be talking about Claude throughout this series, but these patterns apply to any coding agent. The principles are tool-agnostic — the specific model is just where you start.

## 3. Reinforcing Patterns

- Turn off token counters / cost dashboards if they're influencing your behavior
- If your company has a budget concern, frame it as: "A full day of heavy usage costs less than a coffee. The productivity delta is orders of magnitude larger."
- Commit for at least two weeks before evaluating. One bad session isn't data.

## 4. Why What You Were Doing Wasn't Working

You're building a Formula 1 race car with a lawn mower engine. The model is the engine of everything that follows. Weaker models:
- Reason less deeply about complex problems
- Follow multi-step instructions less reliably
- Lose coherence over longer conversations
- Miss nuance in your project's conventions

You wouldn't evaluate your own coding ability on a toy project with no context. Don't evaluate AI capability using the cheapest model.

## 5. Why the Alternative Works Better

The gap between best and second-best isn't incremental — it's qualitative. Better models don't just make fewer mistakes; they think differently. They plan before acting, catch edge cases, and maintain context across long sessions.

Everything else in this series assumes you're running the best available model. If the foundation isn't solid, none of the later steps produce the results they should.

## Notes
- Frame as career investment. This is the way things are going. Others are producing 10X more. If you want to remain in the game, you have to join it.
- If you genuinely cannot afford the best model, use a less expensive one with a TUI. The TUI matters more than the specific model tier.
- This is where the hidden arc starts: best model is step 1 toward it handling spec-driven work later. Don't say that yet.
